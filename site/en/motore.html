Under the bonnet

How a player on the ball thinks

01

Positions

Everyone runs towards his ideal spot — formation, role, ball — at a speed limited by Pace, Acceleration and energy.

02

Pressure

Nearby defenders press the ball carrier, according to Work Rate, energy and instructions.

03

Options

A pass to each team-mate, through ball, dribble, shot, cross: each with a chance of success and a value.

04

Choice

Players with high Decisions almost always choose well. Under pressure and with little Composure, they get it wrong more often.

Morale, condition and friendships come onto the pitch: between two friends the pass arrives a little more often. The full explanation (in Italian) is in docs/03-match-engine.md.
