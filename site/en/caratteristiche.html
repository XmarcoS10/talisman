Pillar 01 · Squad dynamics

Who is friends with whom: psychology is not a fixed number.

Morale goes from 1 to 100, but nobody lives alone: friendships, rivalries, groups by nationality, leaders the rest of the squad listens to. A row in training can turn into a feud.

→
Dressing room graph

See who is in charge, who is isolated and where the discontent starts before it becomes a crisis.

→
Promises that are remembered

Promise a youngster playing time and then leave him in the stands: he remembers, and so does his agent at the next renewal.

dressing roomgraph
Pillar 02 · Tactics and roles

Your choices show on the pitch: no obscure algorithms.

Formations, roles and instructions change the way the team moves. An attacking full-back leaves space behind; a high line invites the ball over the top. You see it on the 2D pitch, not in a table.

→
Player instructions and match plans

Who shoots, who stays back, who man-marks. And plans that kick in on their own: "if we're behind from the 70th minute, attacking mentality".

→
Live match with the analyst

During the game the analyst tells you where you're losing it, and you can change things without stopping everything.

tacticsformation
Pillar 03 · Stories and press

The league tells stories: more than 40 living ones.

Stories grow out of what happens: the crisis, the winning run, the wonderkid, the bogey team that has beaten you for three years. Journalists ask you about those stories.

→
You know what an answer does before you give it

Every answer shows what it moves: a player's morale, the fans, the board's trust.

→
In your language

Stories and press conferences are written in English or Italian. Switch language, and the past ones are rewritten too.

press room
Pillar 04 · Scouting and transfers

You never know everything: the market is a calculated bet.

You only see estimates of other clubs' players, with their uncertainty. Scouts make them more accurate — but some always err in the same direction, and a big signing can turn out a flop.

→
Real negotiations

A hidden price, patience that runs out, instalments, bonuses, sell-on clauses, loans, free transfers.

→
Agents with a memory

If you treated one of their clients badly, they know. And it shows at the table.

The foundations

The pillars of management

Academy and youth league

Every summer a new intake of youngsters arrives, with a preview of who's coming. Now and then, even at the smallest club, an exceptional talent.

Youth league

Real accounts

Tickets, TV rights, sponsors, wages, transfer instalments. Cash month by month and an end-of-season forecast. Financial fair play shows no mercy.

Financial fair play

The board

It gives you a target, but it's a contract: you can ask for two seasons, and you pay for it in trust straight away. When the trust runs out, you're sacked.

Renegotiable targets

Three leagues

Serie A, Serie B with play-offs and play-out, Serie C. Clubs that overspend go into administration, with a points deduction.

Clubs that go bust
